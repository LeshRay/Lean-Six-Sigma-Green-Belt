Lean Six Sigma Green Belt Project
## Contents Claims Process Optimisation

###  Overview
This project focuses on analysing and improving the Home Contents Claims process, specifically addressing inefficiencies between replacement and cash settlement workflows.

The objective is to reduce operational costs, improve turnaround times, enhance customer experience, and strengthen compliance with internal and regulatory standards.

---

### Objective
- Reduce average settlement time by **30%**
- Improve process consistency and compliance
- Minimise financial leakage due to inaccurate settlements
- Enhance customer satisfaction

---

### Problem Statement
The current claims process is experiencing:
- Long settlement turnaround times (up to 89 days)
- High variability in outcomes
- Underutilisation of supplier panels
- Data inaccuracies and inconsistent validation practices
- Increased risk of fraud and overpayment

---

### Key Findings
- Up to **36% of claims** were settled using unvalidated customer quotes
- Discrepancies of up to **98%** between supplier quotes and settlements
- Significant cost leakage due to bypassing supplier channels

**Examples:**
- Sound System: $1,884 (supplier) vs $8,840 (settlement)
- Home Automation: $699 vs $34,587.80 (potential fraud indicator)

---

### Scope
#### In Scope:
- Home contents claims process
- Supplier System usage
- Quoting and settlement workflows
- Data validation and accuracy
- Staff training and process standardisation

#### Out of Scope:
- Building claims
- System integrations
- Third-party/liability claims

---

### Approach (DMAIC)

**Define**
- Identified inefficiencies and cost leakage areas

**Measure**
- Analysed settlement times, discrepancies, and process adherence

**Analyse**
- Root cause analysis on supplier usage and validation gaps

**Improve**
- Proposed:
  - Standardised workflows
  - Supplier-first approach
  - Enhanced training programs
  - Validation controls

**Control**
- Monitoring KPIs
- Continuous improvement framework
- Governance and compliance checks

---

### Expected Outcomes
- Reduced settlement time
- Improved cost control
- Increased supplier utilisation
- Enhanced fraud detection
- Better customer experience
